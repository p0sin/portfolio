# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Astro, static output. Deploy target: a free host, GitHub Pages or Vercel (decide at deploy time; no design impact).

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Arturo Pulido as an AI engineer. They skim quickly, want proof of real projects and skills, and need an easy way to get in touch or obtain a CV.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio that presents Arturo's AI engineering work so a hiring manager can judge his ability and contact him. Success: a recruiter reaches out or downloads his CV.

## Positioning
AI Engineer (hero title). Differentiator, taken from his CV: designs LLM agents end to end and lets code, not the model, make the final call (hybrid quality gates, escalation to a human where reliability matters). Three production systems at SENSO MX: ElevarIA, SofIA and a multi-agent repository maintenance pipeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Language: English only.
- Source of truth for all facts: content/arturopo_cv.pdf.
- Do not publish client names. Do not publish the phone number. Public contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub.
- Open decisions: domain, deploy target (free: GitHub Pages or Vercel).

## Brand Commitments
Name: Arturo Pulido. Visual world chosen with the user: "ink on paper", ASCII art as the visual language, light theme, monospace system type with a serif for display, a single vermilion accent. Hero is an ASCII portrait built from his own photo whose eyes follow the cursor. Architecture diagrams are drawn in ASCII.

## Evidence on Hand
CV with three real projects, skills, education and languages. No testimonials, metrics beyond those in the CV, client logos or screenshots exist. Future work must not fabricate projects, employers, metrics or testimonials; ask for real content.

## Product Principles
- Prove ability with real work, not claims.
- Make contact and CV one obvious step away.
- Respect a recruiter's limited time: the most important facts come first.
- Only state what is true and supplied by Arturo.
